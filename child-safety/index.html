Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Daah Date has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This policy explains our standards, how we respond to violations, and how to reach us. It applies to every person who uses the Daah Date app, in every country.

1. What is prohibited

The following are strictly prohibited on Daah Date, with no exceptions:

  • Creating an account or profile for, or on behalf of, anyone under the age of 18
  • Uploading, sending, requesting, or soliciting any sexual content involving a minor, in any form, including images, video, text, or illustrated/AI-generated content
  • Any attempt to groom, sexually exploit, sexually solicit, or endanger a minor
  • Any attempt to use Daah Date to contact, meet, or arrange to meet a minor for a sexual purpose
  • Sharing links to, or directing another user toward, child sexual abuse material (CSAM) hosted anywhere, on or off this App

Daah Date is restricted to users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to hold an account.

2. How to report a concern

If you see, receive, or suspect any of the above:

  • In the App: use the Report button on any profile, message, or photo. Reports reach our moderation team directly and are reviewed as a priority.
  • By email: safety@daahdate.com
  • You do not need to be the person affected to report something — anyone can report suspected CSAE.

We will never disclose to the reported user that they were reported, and reporting in good faith will never result in any penalty to the reporting user.

3. What happens when we find a violation

When we have actual knowledge of a violation of this policy:

  • The account responsible is permanently banned immediately, without warning.
  • The content involved is removed from the App.
  • We preserve the evidence required for law enforcement referral, consistent with applicable law.
  • Where legally required, we report confirmed child sexual abuse material (CSAM) to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) (ncmec.org) or, for users outside the United States, the equivalent authority in the relevant country.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement investigations into CSAE to the extent required and permitted by law.

4. Our compliance commitment

Daah Date self-certifies that it:

  • Maintains and publishes these standards, prohibiting CSAE in all forms (this document).
  • Provides an in-app mechanism for users to report CSAE and other safety concerns (the Report button described above).
  • Takes appropriate action — including removal of content and termination of accounts — upon obtaining actual knowledge of CSAE, consistent with this policy and applicable law.
  • Complies with applicable child safety laws, including reporting confirmed CSAM to NCMEC or the relevant authority where legally required.
