You can request deletion of your Daah Date account and all associated data from here, even if you no longer have the app installed.

From inside the app (fastest)

Open Daah Date → Profile → Privacy & safety → Delete my account. This immediately and permanently deletes your profile, photos, matches, and messages. This cannot be undone.

From the web (if you can't open the app)

Send an email to support@daahdate.com from the email address your account is registered under, with the subject line "Delete my account". Include:

  • The email address your Daah Date account uses
  • A short confirmation that you want your account permanently deleted

We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days of receiving a verified request. Some information may be retained longer where required for legal, safety, or Child Safety Standards enforcement reasons — see our Privacy Policy for details.

For details on what is deleted and what, if anything, is retained and why, see the Privacy Policy.
