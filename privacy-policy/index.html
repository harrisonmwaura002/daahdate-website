Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Daah Date ("we," "us," "our"), operated by Harrison Githinji Mwaura of Nairobi, Kenya, who is the data controller for your information, collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Daah Date mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and password (your password is never stored in readable form — it's handled securely by our authentication provider).

Profile information. Your name, age, bio, gender, orientation, country, and any prompts or interests you choose to add — all of which you provide and can edit at any time.

Photos. Any profile photo you choose to upload. Uploaded photos are checked against a short set of rules (for example, minimum image quality and duplicate detection) before they're accepted; some checks may also be reviewed by a human moderator.

Verification selfie (optional). If you choose to verify your profile, you submit a single selfie photo following an on-screen pose prompt. This photo is stored privately and is reviewed by a human moderator only, to confirm it matches your profile photo — it is never used for automated facial recognition or any other automated biometric processing, and it is not shared with anyone besides our moderators.

Location. If you choose to share it, your device's approximate GPS coordinates (rounded to roughly a 1 km area — never your exact position), used to:

  • show other users a rounded distance to you (e.g., "3 km away"), and
  • show your approximate position on the in-app map, but only while you are online in the App. We do not track or update your location in the background; it is refreshed only when you have the App open and have already shared your location, at most once an hour.

We also use your approximate location (or your selected country) to detect and let you set your country, which is shown to other users and used for the "My country / Continents / Worldwide" matching preference.

Activity data. Who you swipe on and in which direction, your matches, and the content of messages you send within the App, including text, photos, videos, and voice messages.

Device and push notification data. A device-specific push token, used solely to deliver notifications about new matches and messages to your device. You can turn match and message notifications on or off independently in the App's settings.

Safety reports. If you report another user, we collect the reason and any details you provide, along with which profile was reported.

We do not collect continuous background location tracking, contacts list access, or browsing history outside the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Show your profile to other users, and show you theirs
  • Calculate and display approximate distance, and show approximate position on the map, only for users who are currently online and have shared their location
  • Enable matching and messaging between users who both express interest
  • Send push notifications for new matches and messages, if you've enabled them
  • Review uploaded photos and verification selfies for compliance with our photo rules
  • Investigate safety reports and take action against accounts that violate our community standards or Child Safety Standards (see below)
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the App

We do not use your information to serve third-party advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. How We Share Your Information

With other users. Your name, age, bio, photo, approximate distance, country, and (if verified) your verified badge are visible to other users as part of normal App functionality. Messages are visible only to you and the person you're matched with.

With service providers. We use Supabase, a third-party database and infrastructure provider, to store and process App data securely. Supabase acts as a data processor on our behalf and is bound by its own security and privacy obligations. Push notifications are delivered via Expo's push notification service, which receives only the device token and notification content needed to deliver the message — not your other profile data.

For safety and legal reasons. We may access or disclose information if required by law, or if we believe in good faith that doing so is necessary to protect the safety of our users or the public, investigate fraud or abuse, enforce our terms, or respond to a legal request — including, where applicable, reporting confirmed child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or an equivalent authority, as described in our Child Safety Standards policy.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. Blocking, Reporting, and Moderation

If you block another user, they will no longer be able to see your profile or contact you, and this is enforced at the database level, not just hidden in the app interface. If you report another user, that report is reviewed by us; the reported user is not automatically notified that they were reported. Photos and verification selfies may also be reviewed by a human moderator as part of normal operation of the App.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including:

  • Row Level Security (database-enforced access rules ensuring users can only access data they're authorized to see)
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the App and our servers
  • Private storage for sensitive files (such as verification selfies and chat attachments), accessible only via short-lived, access-controlled links
  • Access to sensitive data restricted to what's operationally necessary

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to or stored by the App.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You can, at any time, within the App:

Delete my account. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, photos, matches, and messages. This cannot be undone, and we cannot recover this information for you afterward.

  • Edit or delete your profile information, bio, photos, prompts, and interests
  • Turn location sharing on or off
  • Turn match and message notifications on or off independently
  • Block or unblock other users
  • Report profiles that violate our community standards
  • Sign out of your account
  • Permanently delete your account — go to Profile → Privacy & safety →

If you can't open the App (for example, you've uninstalled it), you can also request account deletion from the web at:

https://daahdate.com/delete-account

We will process a web-submitted deletion request within 30 days, except where we're required to retain certain information for legal or safety purposes (for example, records of safety reports involving your account, or confirmed Child Safety Standards violations).

Your data-protection rights. Depending on where you live (including under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 and, for users in the EU or UK, the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, email support@daahdate.com. We will respond within the time the law requires. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya, or to the data-protection authority in your country.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your profile information, photos, messages, and verification selfie within 30 days. We may retain limited information related to safety reports, Child Safety Standards enforcement, or other legal obligations for longer, where necessary.

8. Children's Privacy & Child Safety Standards

Daah Date is intended for users aged 18 and older only. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will delete their account and associated data. We currently rely on self-reported age at signup and do not perform independent age verification.

Daah Date has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Our full commitments, reporting process, and point of contact are published separately in our Child Safety Standards policy, which forms part of this Privacy Policy by reference.

9. International Data Transfers

Our database infrastructure is hosted with Supabase in the European Union (Ireland, AWS region eu-west-1). If you are located outside that region, your information will be transferred to and processed there. Supabase is bound by data-protection commitments as our data processor, and we only transfer the data needed to run the App.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify users through the App or by other reasonable means before the changes take effect.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at:

support@daahdate.com
